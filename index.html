Underwater Soft Robot — Mechatronics Design

2020 – 2026
PhD Thesis, GRASP Lab, University of Pennsylvania
Mechanical design Custom PCB Embedded firmware

Mechatronics design for a jet-propelled underwater soft robot, composed of two main subsystems: a main body that generates jet propulsion force, and a swivel nozzle for active steering.

  • Main body integrates a soft exoskeleton with a custom tendon-driven actuation system.
  • 2-DOF swivel nozzle enables precise directional control of the propulsion jet.
  • Embedded firmware on customized PCB handles onboard sensing and actuation control.
  • Full assembly engineered for waterproofing and mechanical robustness under underwater operating conditions.

Underwater Soft Robot — Fluid Dynamics Simulation

2025 – 2026
PhD Thesis, GRASP Lab, University of Pennsylvania
Fluid dynamics Simulation System identification

Developed a custom simulation to model the dynamics of the jet-propelled underwater soft robot, serving as the training environment for reinforcement learning and reducing reliance on physical experimentation.

  • Built on a modified Newton-Euler formulation with time-dependent parameters derived from data-driven surrogate models.
  • Captures jet force, drag force, and added-mass effects as functions of time.
  • Calibrated against experimentally collected trajectories to ensure simulation fidelity.

Underwater Soft Robot — RL Navigation Control

2025 – 2026
PhD Thesis, GRASP Lab, University of Pennsylvania
Reinforcement learning Sim-to-real Navigation

Reinforcement-learning-based navigation policy trained in simulation and deployed and validated on real hardware.

  • Position-tracking policy trained entirely in simulation, then validated in simulation prior to hardware deployment.
  • Applied domain randomization over dynamics parameters, observation noise, latency, and environmental disturbances to enable sim-to-real transfer.
  • Deployed the policy zero-shot on physical hardware, demonstrating successful sim-to-real transfer for position tracking.
