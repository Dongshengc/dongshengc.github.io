About Me

I'm a recent robotics Ph.D. graduate from the GRASP Lab at the University of Pennsylvania. During my Ph.D., I developed a jet-propelled underwater soft robot. My work covered the full development cycle: from mechanical design, custom PCB design/fabrication, and embedded firmware, to an RL-based navigation policy deployed and validated on real hardware — full-stack, end to end.

I am a full-stack robotics engineer who loves bridging the gap between hardware and software. My current interest lies in using reinforcement learning to solve messy, real-world physical challenges where standard analytical models fail. I'm currently looking for robotics engineering roles focused on hardware-software integration, reinforcement learning, and embodied AI.

When I'm not building robots, you can usually find me working out or practicing my guitar technique.
